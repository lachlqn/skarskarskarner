How to contact almost anyone

August ??, 2025

One of the highest upside things you can do is reach out to people who are: 1. in the position that you want to be in 2. can help you get there, or 3. are on the same journey you are. This applies to founders, angel investors, recruiters, execs and ayone in between. But how do you reach these people?

Cold Outreach

"Cold outreach is the process of reaching out to someone who you don't know and who you don't have a connection to" - Cursor autocomplete. At first, I cringed at cold outreach. But then I put myself in the shoes of someone I am reaching out to, where a) I have a problem I need fixing and b) someone is reaching out to me directly to solve it for me - this is a win-win.

But don't be fooled, cold outreach can be shit. You need to be genuine, specific and straight to the point. Which brings me to point 1.

Do your research

Does this person have the problem that you are trying to solve? Identify that - and this is different in each domain. Founders are dying for good talent. Angel investors are looking for good startups. Recruiters are looking for good talent. Execs are looking people to nurture and pass on the good grace that has been given to them. In 2025, research is not hard to do. Personal brands are the rage and people are posting content publicly. Watch, read, listen to what they're putting out and decide if you can help. Now if you had the chance, what would you say?

Be personal and succinct

Anyone can smell a templated message from a mile away and AI has only made this worse. Mass messaging will not get your messages opened. Take what you've learnt from your research and make it personal, short and sweet. My effectiveness with this method was 8/10 responses, far higher than typical cold outreach.
